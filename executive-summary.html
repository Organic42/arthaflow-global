The Problem
99.7% don't export

India makes world-class products. Almost none ship overseas.

India has 57M MSME manufacturers — yet only 0.3% export. Not because of product quality, but because export infrastructure doesn't exist for them: 40+ documents per shipment, ₹50K+ consultant fees, no access to verified buyers, and fragmented logistics. Most give up before their first container leaves the port.

The Solution
● Live MVP

An enterprise dashboard + AI + partner network — end-to-end.

  • AI Document Generator — export docs in 30 sec vs 3 days (vs ₹50K consultant)
  • Export Saathi — AI advisor in Hindi, Marathi, English; grounded in real trade data
  • Manufacturer Dashboard — catalogue, vault, buyers, shipments, readiness
  • Partner Network — warehousing, freight, finance (first partnership signed)
Why Now

Four tailwinds just converged.

  • Policy — Govt's $2T exports-by-2030 target is funded national policy
  • China + 1 — Buyers de-risking; demand searching for verified Indian supply
  • AI inflection — Documentation friction is automatable for the first time
  • MSME digital — Post-UPI, factory owners are smartphone-native
