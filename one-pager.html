The Problem

India's MSMEs cannot access global trade.

  • 99.7% of 57M Indian MSMEs never export — not for quality but for infrastructure.
  • Exporting demands 40+ documents, verified buyers, and coordinated logistics.
  • Result: $800B+ in unrealized export potential locked inside Indian factories.
The Solution

One AI-powered platform. Every export task. Any Indian language.

  • AI Document Generator — export docs in 30 sec, grounded in real data.
  • Export Saathi — AI advisor in Hindi, Marathi, English (first of its kind).
  • Buyer Discovery — verified international importers matched by capability.
  • Ops Dashboard — compliance, shipments, financing, tracking in one place.
