The Problem

99.7% of Indian Manufacturers Don't Export

Not because their products aren't good enough — because the export infrastructure doesn't exist for them.

📄
Documentation Nightmare
IEC, HS codes, shipping bills, certificates of origin — 40+ documents per shipment. Most give up here.
🔍
No Buyer Access
Zero visibility into who's buying, where, or how to reach verified international buyers. Listing boards aren't matchmakers.
🚢
Logistics Overwhelm
Freight, customs, insurance, incoterms — 15+ decisions per shipment. One mistake = delayed cargo + penalties.

"I spent 2 years trying to figure out how to export. I gave up after the documentation."
— Manufacturer, Pune

02 — The Problem
Why Now

Four Tailwinds Just Converged

The export opportunity isn't new. The ability to capture it — at MSME scale, profitably — is. Four things became true in the last 24 months.

🏛️
Policy Mandate
Govt targeting $2T exports by 2030. District Export Hubs, RoDTEP & PLI actively pushing MSMEs outward — now funded national policy.
🌐
China + 1
Global buyers de-risking from China. India is the #1 alternative — but they can't find verified Indian suppliers. Demand is hunting for supply.
🤖
AI Inflection
Documentation that needed a consultant 24 months ago now costs ₹0 in marginal compute. The core friction became automatable for the first time.
📱
MSME Digitization
Post-UPI, GST & ONDC, India's smallest manufacturers are digital-native. A factory owner in Nashik runs his business on a smartphone.

The pieces existed separately for years. In 2024–25 they locked into place together. The company that builds the rails now owns the next decade of Indian exports.
